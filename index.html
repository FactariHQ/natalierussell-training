How I coach

Fitness doesn't need to be a personality. It needs a plan you can follow on a busy week, and someone checking that it's working.

Strength first

Squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. Getting stronger at the basics makes everything else easier, from stairs to sport to picking up your kids.

Measured progress

Every session is logged. You'll see your numbers go up in weight, reps and how hard it feels, so progress isn't a guess.

Built for real life

Two sessions a week or five. Full gym or a pair of dumbbells. The program fits your schedule, your equipment and your history.

Your first month

The first four weeks set the baseline. After that, programs run in 4-week blocks with a review at the end of each one.

  1. Consult

    A free 20-minute call about your goals, schedule, injuries and what's worked before.

  2. Assess

    A movement screen and baseline numbers, so we know where you're starting.

  3. Train

    Your first program, coached closely while you learn the lifts and the logging.

  4. Review

    We compare against your baseline and set the targets for your next block.

Questions

If yours isn't here, ask in the consult.

I've never lifted. Is this for me?

Yes. Most clients start as beginners. We begin with movements you can do well today and add load gradually as your technique holds up.

I have an old injury. Can I still train?

Usually, yes. Tell me about it in the consult. If you're under a doctor's or physical therapist's care, I'll work within their guidance.

How often do I need to train?

Two sessions a week is enough to make steady progress. Three is better if your schedule allows it.

Do you help with nutrition?

I cover the basics that support training, like protein, hydration and sleep. For detailed meal plans or medical nutrition needs, I refer to a registered dietitian.

What's your cancellation policy?

Reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice and the session carries over. Confirm policy
